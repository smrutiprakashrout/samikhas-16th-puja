# PRD — Samikshya's 16th Birthday Puja invitation (mobile web)
**Goal:** a premium, calm, scroll-driven invitation opened from a WhatsApp link on a phone. Replaces the printed card's digital twin; not a copy of it.
**Audience:** family and elders; 99% phones. Elders must find the date, time, plain-text address and phone numbers without effort.
**Must show:** Ganesha invocation + shloka · her 16th Birthday Puja (name, photo) · invitation line · family (parents, grandparents) · Sunday 29 Nov 2026, 7:00 PM onwards · venue Sapnpur, Sisua, Salepur, Cuttack (plain text + embedded map + directions) · RSVP numbers 9853993661 / 7735172417 · closing line + share.
**Experience:** gate (sandalwood doors open into light) → sand → Ganesha + shloka → hero (arch + photo + "16th") → invitation → family → date/time → venue → RSVP → closing. Background music after the first tap, mute always available.
**Look:** warm sand + marigold, "sandalwood" (docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md). **Stack:** Vite, vanilla JS, anime.js v4. **Deadline:** Sat 21 Nov 2026.
**Non-goals:** tablet/desktop layouts, languages other than English + the Devanagari shloka, any backend.
